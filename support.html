AN ETHICAL STATEMENT OF GRATITUDE
Every voluntary contribution is received with sincere gratitude and respect for the person who gives it.
Your generosity is not an obligation, and no visitor must pay to read material that has been expressly made available for free. A contribution is an expression of support, not a purchase of influence, access to confidential information or agreement with every statement published in this archive.
Where contributions are received, their intended purpose will be described transparently. We will not promise outcomes we cannot guarantee, misrepresent the legal status of an organisation or imply that a gift is tax deductible without the necessary verification.
Whether you purchase a book, share a publication, contribute voluntarily or simply read, thank you for giving this body of creative work the opportunity to reach another person.

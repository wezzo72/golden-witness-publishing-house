The author as witness

Barran Dodger, the literary name of Dr Richard William McLean, understands his vocation as that of a witness: someone who writes spiritual testimony, creative literature, and a public record of what he says was done to him.

That understanding is his. It is not presented here as a fact established by this house. Theological claims, prophetic language, and allegations about institutions remain his voice. A title is not a verdict. An appearance in the catalogue is not a finding.

The Golden Witness is a new publishing front. It does not replace the older archives. It points to them.
